# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Adults in Ragusa and its province (Modica, Vittoria, Comiso, Scicli) going through a difficult period — anxiety, stress, relational difficulties, life crises — who are looking for a psychologist/psychotherapist nearby or online. Their job on the site: understand who the therapist is, trust her, and book the free first consultation (or send a message) without friction.

## Product Purpose
Professional website of Dott.ssa Lorena Di Pasquale, psicologa e psicoterapeuta. Success = a visitor books the free first consultation via Calendly, writes via the contact form/WhatsApp, or calls.

## Positioning
- Psychoanalytic training (Istituto Italiano di Psicoterapia Psicoanalitica di Palermo) plus certified specialist techniques: TFP (Livello A e B), EMDR (1° e 2° livello), Facilitatore Mindfulness.
- First consultation is free.
- Sessions both in the studio (Corso Italia 398/b, Ragusa) and online.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive mostly from Google local search ("psicologa ragusa", "bonus psicologo"), often on mobile. Booking happens inside an embedded Calendly widget gated by cookie consent; messages go through Formspree; urgent contact via WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site (index.html, styles.css, script.js) deployed on GitHub Pages at dipasqualelorena.it. No build step.
- Calendly inline widget (cookie-gated) and Formspree form (endpoint xnngjrkq) must keep working.
- All SEO meta tags, Open Graph, and JSON-LD structured data must be preserved.
- Google Analytics with consent mode; cookie banner and settings modal must keep working.
- Existing copy and content are binding: may be reorganised, not rewritten in substance; no new claims.
- The Bonus Psicologo popup is not binding; the information (adheres to Bonus Psicologo INPS) must stay prominent.

## Brand Commitments
- Name as "Dott.ssa Di Pasquale Lorena" / "Dott.ssa Lorena Di Pasquale"; Italian language only.
- Tone: empathic, collaborative, professional.
- Visual direction preference (chosen 2026-10-13): the category standard for a psychologist's site, executed at full craft, not an experimental world. Quality bar: Unobravo and Serenis (Italian online-therapy platforms).

## Product Principles
1. Lower the threshold: the path to a free first conversation is always one step away.
2. Trust through competence shown plainly — real credentials, no hype.
3. Calm, never clinical; warm, never saccharine.
4. Respect privacy: consent before third-party scripts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Visitors may be anxious or distressed: readable type sizes, high contrast, no aggressive motion, reduced-motion respected, fully keyboard accessible.
